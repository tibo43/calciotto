Features

Everything your game needs

No more counting thumbs in the chat. One link for the whole group, from sign-up to the final ranking.

  • One tap to sign up

    Say you're in, or drop out, in a second. Everyone sees who's playing and how many places are left.

  • Balanced teams

    Two fair sides built from everyone's positions in your sport, ready before the game. The admin can still swap players by hand.

  • Predictions

    Call the result before the game and climb the predictions leaderboard over the season.

  • Star of the Match

    After the game, everyone votes for the best player. The winner gets the star.

  • Stats and rankings

    Wins, points and stars for every player, season after season.

  • Push notifications

    Match reminders, prediction results and the Star of the Match, straight to your phone. Turn off what you don't need.

How it works

Three steps to game day

Your group admin creates the group and shares an invite. After that, it takes about a minute.

  1. 01

    Join your group

    Open the invite link or enter the code from your group admin. Each group plays one sport.

  2. 02

    Sign up for the game

    Tap Sign up when the next game opens. Plans changed? Withdraw just as fast and free your place.

  3. 03

    Play and vote

    Play, check the score, vote for the Star of the Match and watch the rankings move.

Install

Put it on your home screen

No app store needed. Install it from your browser to open it full screen and get notifications.

iPhone

Safari
  1. 01Open club.agoran.app in Safari.
  2. 02Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen.
  3. 03Open Agoran from your home screen and turn on notifications.

On iPhone, notifications only work in the app opened from the home screen.

Android

Chrome
  1. 01Open club.agoran.app in Chrome.
  2. 02Tap Install when it's offered, or open the menu ⋮ and choose Install app.
  3. 03Open Agoran and allow notifications.

No Install option? Choose Add to Home screen in the same menu.
